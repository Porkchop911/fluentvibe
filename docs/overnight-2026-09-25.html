The questions they asked

Each model asked one question, listing every open number with a proposal tied to a step of the guide, and checked deck feasibility itself.

Qwen3.8-27B100 s

  1. Bead slurry 20 µL/well (0.2 mg)
  2. Washes 3 × 20 µL 1X B&W (literal "equal volume")
  3. 2X B&W 40 µL — "twice the original volume"
  4. Probe 40 µL, 15 min at room temperature

Qwen3.8-Flash6 min

  1. Bead slurry 20 µL/well (0.2 mg ≈ 100 pmol oligo)
  2. Washes 3 × 60 µL ("practical minimum for 96-channel aspiration")
  3. Probe 40 µL; coated washes 2 × 80 µL; final 40 µL
  4. Totals: 36 mL 1X B&W fits one 60 mL SBS reservoir; 43 mL waste

Slip: 20 µL 2X B&W instead of 40, and "→ 80 µL total" does not add up. The benchmark auto-answered "yes"; a person answering would catch it. That is what the question step is for.

Protocol quality

  • Every step of sections 1–2 is present in both answered protocols: resuspend, beads, 3 washes on the magnet, 2X B&W, probe, the 15-minute incubation (operator step, since the deck has no rotator), coated washes, final resuspension.
  • Flash follows the reagent rule: beads, 2X B&W and probe by the FCA from slim troughs, 1X B&W by the MCA from an SBS reservoir.
  • 27B stamps beads and probe with the MCA from 96-well source plates, which someone would have to fill by hand first. The labware skill now says so (afec83a).
  • Both are valid but slow on the instrument: much column-by-column FCA work. Supernatant tips are reused across columns, which is acceptable because before hybridization every well holds the same beads and probe.
